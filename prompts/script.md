Generate a script for a video in json format matching the following:

[
    {
        "narration": "text describing what the narrator is saying",
        "image": "description of what's happening in the video",
        "characters": [
            "character1",
            "character2"
        ]
    }
]

Here's an example:

[
    {
        "narration": "Jane was having a rough day at school. She'd gotten bullied again.",
        "image": "Jane is walking down a school hallway, crying.",
        "characters": [
            "Jane"
        ]
    },
    {
        "narration": "During lunch that day, John had called her names and hurt her feelings.",
        "image": "John yells at Jane in a school cafeteria.",
        "characters": [
            "Jane",
            "John"
        ]
    }
]

Each line represents one story beat: a line of dialogue, a scene description, or any other component that warrants its own imagery. The narration will be used to produce the narration, and the image will be used as a prompt to produce an image of the scene. Your story can be about whatever you want it to be about, but I have some requests to make it appeal to more people.

PROSE
Your prose should be simple, unambiguous, and straightforward. When in doubt, tell, don't show. We want the audience to know what's going on here without needing to think. Keep the sentences short and declarative. We want everyone to understand what's happening in our story, even people that don't speak or understand English very well.

STORY STRUCTURE
Your story should be easy to understand. The opening should be catchy and hook the audience immediately with its striking visuals. You should center your story on an easily digestible moral premise. There should be an easy to understand story arc that ends with some sort of lesson. You should state the moral of the story explicitly at or near the end.

Any individual conflict or story arc should rise to a climax over the course of the story before resolving at the end. When in doubt, keep raising the stakes. You should never state the moral of the story any time other than the last story beat. You should avoid resolving tension in the plot until the end of the video. No recurring character should be purely instrumental. Give every character a memorable goal, pesonality trait, or quirk to distinguish them.

TONE
You should try to invoke classically American and nostalgic imagery.

PACING
Each story beat should be about 5-10s (12-30 words) long. There should be a total of 6-9 beats in each story.

Your characters need to be original and not from any existing media. You can use old or new characters, scenarios, and locations. Just try to make it engaging and original.
